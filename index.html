About KIWIN'S

Glen A. Wilson High School KIWIN'S, part of the Goldstone Division, has proudly served our school and community since 2014. Guided by our advisor, Mrs. B., our club is committed to leadership, service, and friendship. Members participate in Adopt-A-Highway, Stuff-A-Bus, Rose Float building, beach cleanups, food drives, and many other volunteer opportunities throughout the year.
