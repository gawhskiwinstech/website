About KIWIN'S

Glen A. Wilson High School KIWIN'S is a student-led service organization dedicated to leadership, volunteerism, and friendship. Since 2014, our members have proudly served our school and local community through beach cleanups, Adopt-A-Highway, Rose Float building, Stuff-A-Bus, food drives, and countless other volunteer opportunities.
