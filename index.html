OUR PURPOSE

What We Do

Since 2014, GAWHS KIWIN'S has brought students together through service, leadership, and friendship. As part of the Goldstone Division, we create opportunities to give back to our school and local community.

🌳

Community Service

From Adopt-A-Highway and Stuff-A-Bus to Rose Float building and other volunteer projects, we work together to make a meaningful difference.

✦

Leadership

KIWIN'S gives students opportunities to lead, take initiative, and grow through hands-on service and real-world experiences.

♥

Friendship

Beyond service, we build a supportive community where members form lasting friendships and encourage one another.

Guided by our advisor, Mrs. B., we support one another and strive to make a positive impact through every act of service.
